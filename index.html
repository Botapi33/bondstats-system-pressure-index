Methodology

The index is a current sovereign-rate pressure snapshot, not a credit rating, crisis probability or trading signal. It combines yield level, move intensity, stress breadth and upward pressure. The construction is deliberately fixed and transparent so changes in the reading reflect the market data rather than changes in the model.
