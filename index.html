METHODOLOGY

What the index actually measures

This is a sovereign-rate pressure snapshot, not a crisis probability, credit rating or trading signal.

40%

Yield Level

Scores the median 10-year yield across fresh daily markets. Higher long-term financing costs contribute more pressure.

25%

Move Intensity

Scores the median absolute one-observation yield move. Large moves in either direction indicate greater rate-market pressure.

20%

Stress Breadth

Measures how many fresh markets move at least 3 basis points. Broad moves matter more than a single isolated outlier.

15%

Upward Pressure

Measures the share of fresh markets with yields rising at least 1 basis point, capturing broad increases in sovereign financing costs.

Eligibility rule. Only non-fallback observations marked Daily with staleness of seven days or less are included. Monthly and stale data remain visible in the BondStats master source but cannot influence the current score.
